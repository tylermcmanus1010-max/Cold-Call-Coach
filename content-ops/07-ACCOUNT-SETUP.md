# PART 0 — THE DECISIONS (already made)

## The name: COLD OPEN

**Why this one.** "Cold open" is the film/TV term for starting a scene mid-action
with zero setup — no titles, no preamble, straight into the moment. That is
simultaneously (a) literally what a cold call is and (b) precisely the thesis of
this entire page: kill the throat-clearing, start mid-motion. A name that means
two true things at once is worth more than a clever one that means nothing. It's
8 characters, it's sayable out loud, it has no numbers, no underscores, and no
year in it, and it doesn't reference a person — which matters because the page is
faceless and you may eventually want to sell it.

## The audience

**Primary: SDRs and BDRs, 22–30, zero to three years in, at B2B SaaS and
services companies, US-based, carrying a dial quota of 40–100 a day.**

That's the target because: they cold call as their literal daily job so the
content is immediately usable; they're 22–30, which is the short-form native
band; their compensation depends on getting better, so they buy training with
their own money without needing approval; they are *anxious*, which is the
highest-intent emotional state a buyer can be in; and they're already on TikTok
during the 4pm dial-block lull.

**Secondary: sales managers and SDR team leads** — they buy for teams, which is
where the actual revenue is. They'll find you through their reps sharing clips.
Never target them directly; targeting managers makes the content boring and the
reps stop sharing it.

**Tertiary: solo founders doing their own outbound.** Highest urgency, worst
skills, will buy anything that works. Free upside, don't optimize for them.

## The point of view

> **Cold calling is a set of learnable mechanics that live almost entirely in the
> first 8 seconds. It is not a personality trait, and it is not a mindset problem.**

## The enemy

> **Motivational sales content.** The grindset guys selling belief instead of
> technique. "Just dial more." "You gotta want it." Every piece of content we make
> is implicitly an argument that the mechanics are knowable and that telling
> someone to believe in themselves is what people say when they don't know the
> mechanics.

This is what makes the tension renewable. We never have to manufacture a
conflict — we have a real one, we're right about it, and it generates infinite
content. Every motivational sales post on the internet is a prompt for us.

## Brand kit

**Colors:**
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background | `#0B0B0C` (near-black) |
| Text | `#FAFAFA` (off-white) |
| Accent | `#FF3B30` (on-air red) |
| De-emphasis | `#8A8A8E` (grey) |

Red, specifically, because every B2B sales account on earth uses corporate blue.
Red reads as recording, on-air, alert — it's thematically correct *and* it's the
only way to be visually distinct in a feed full of navy gradients.

**Fonts** (all free, Google Fonts):
- On-screen text / headlines: **Archivo Black** — heavy enough to read at
  thumbnail size, which is the only size that matters
- Captions / body: **Inter SemiBold**
- Numbers, timers, scoreboards: **JetBrains Mono** — the monospace look sells the
  "real data" framing

**Avatar:** Black square. One centered red filled circle — a record dot. Nothing
else. No text, no waveform, no icon. It has to be legible at 40 pixels in a
comment thread, and at 40px anything more than one shape becomes mud. Make it
once at 1024×1024 PNG and use the identical file on all five platforms.

**Banner (X, YouTube, Facebook):** Black. Left-aligned in Archivo Black, white:
`COLD OPEN`. Below it in grey Inter: `the first 8 seconds decide the call`. Red
record dot to the left of the wordmark. Keep everything inside the center 1235×338
safe zone on YouTube or it gets cropped on mobile.

# PART 2 — TIKTOK (~10 min)

Highest discovery value. Get it aging immediately — account age is a real ranking
input.

1. `tiktok.com/signup` → **Use phone or email** → sign up with your new Gmail.
   Do *not* use "Continue with Google" — it links identities in ways you can't
   easily unlink later.
2. Birthday: your real one. Getting flagged as under-18 permanently blocks
   monetization and is a nightmare to reverse.
3. Set username to your handle. Display name `Cold Open`. Upload avatar.
4. **Bio** (80 char limit — this is exactly 71):
   ```
   The first 8 seconds decide the call. Mechanics, not motivation.
   ```
5. Add your link. *(Bio links unlock at 1,000 followers on TikTok — if it's not
   available yet, put the URL in your bio text as plain text for now.)*
6. **Settings → Manage account → Switch to Business Account** → category
   **Education**. Not "Business Services" — Education gets better organic
   treatment and is accurate.
7. **Settings → Privacy → Downloads: ON.** Deliberately. This is the IShowSpeed
   lesson — letting people clip you is free distribution and you want it.
8. **Settings → Privacy → Comments → Filter keywords:** add slurs and spam terms.
   You want an open comment section but not a moderation job.

# PART 6 — THE 7-DAY WARM-UP

Do not skip this. A brand-new account posting at full volume on day one pattern-
matches to a bot and gets reach-limited before you've published anything good.
The suppression is silent and you will not be told it happened.

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | **Zero posts.** On each platform: follow 20–30 sales accounts, watch content in the niche to completion, like and comment genuinely. You are training the algorithm's model of who you are. |
| 3–4 | 1 post/day per platform. Use scripts 3, 1 — the thesis and the first teardown. |
| 5–7 | 2 posts/day. Start the 30-day series (script 5) on day 5 so the scoreboard begins with a full head of steam. |
| 8+ | Full cadence per `03-PLATFORM-PLAYBOOKS.md`. |

During days 1–2, log the accounts you follow. Their comment sections are your
first content backlog — every objection someone raises in a comment is a script.
