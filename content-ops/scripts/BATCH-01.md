# Batch 01 — First 30 Scripts

Niche: cold calling / sales (Option A). If the niche changes, the hook families
and beat structures port directly — only the specifics get rewritten.

Format per script: **Hook** (chosen from 3) · **Beats** · **Payoff** · **Final frame**

Pillars: `TEARDOWN` · `OBJECTION` · `SCRIPT` · `PSYCH` · `SERIES` · `CONTRARIAN`

---

## 1. TEARDOWN — "The 8 seconds that killed the call"
**Hook** (visual cold open): Waveform on screen, call audio already playing. Rep
says "Hi, is this a bad time?" Text slams in at 1.5s: *"He already lost."*
**Beats:** 0–3 audio plays raw · 3–8 freeze on waveform, "this question gives away
the frame" · 8–15 why permission-asking openers invite the no · 15–25 the same
opener rebuilt · 25–32 replay both back to back.
**Payoff:** the A/B replay. Viewer hears the difference, doesn't just get told.
**Final frame:** the rebuilt line on screen, silent. Loops into the original.

## 2. OBJECTION — "Not interested"
**Hook** (contradiction): "'Not interested' isn't an objection. It's a reflex, and
you're arguing with a reflex."
**Beats:** it fires before they've processed anything · arguing confirms the frame ·
the move: agree, then pivot to a specific · script line delivered twice at speed ·
what to do with the 2 seconds of silence after.
**Payoff:** exact words, on screen, repeatable.
**Final frame:** the line, big, no VO.

## 3. PSYCH — "The first 8 seconds of a cold call are the first 3 seconds of a TikTok"
**Hook** (contradiction): "Cold calling and going viral are the same skill and
nobody says it."
**Beats:** both are a stranger deciding whether to keep paying attention · both
lose most people in the first breath · both die from preamble · the shared fix:
open mid-motion, no throat-clearing.
**Payoff:** the parallel is genuinely true and nobody frames it this way — this is
the page's thesis post.
**Final frame:** split screen, phone / feed, same clock running.

## 4. SCRIPT — "The 11-word opener"
**Hook** (specificity): "Eleven words. That's the whole opener. I've tested about
forty."
**Beats:** count them on screen as spoken · why each word is load-bearing · what
happens if you add "quick question" (it dies) · deliver at real speed.
**Payoff:** the actual line, on screen, copyable.
**Final frame:** the 11 words, numbered.

## 5. SERIES — "100 cold calls in 30 days. Day 1."
**Hook** (stakes): "100 cold calls in 30 days. Everything gets posted, including
the ones where I get destroyed."
**Beats:** state the rules · today's number · the worst moment, unedited · the
scoreboard graphic.
**Payoff:** real audio of a real failure. Nobody posts this.
**Final frame:** scoreboard. `1/100`. Same graphic every day — becomes the brand.

## 6. TEARDOWN — "He said 4 words and closed it"
**Hook** (open loop): "...and then he said four words and the whole thing turned."
**Beats:** play up to the turn · stop · make them guess (comment bait, honest
kind) · reveal · explain the mechanism.
**Payoff:** the four words + why they work.
**Final frame:** the four words. Comment prompt: "what would you have said?"

## 7. OBJECTION — "Send me an email"
**Hook** (loss framing): "'Send me an email' is a no wearing a suit. Here's how to
tell the difference."
**Beats:** two versions of the same phrase, different tone · the diagnostic
question · what to send if it's real · what to say if it isn't.
**Payoff:** the one-line diagnostic.
**Final frame:** the question on screen.

## 8. CONTRARIAN — "Stop building rapport"
**Hook** (contradiction): "Rapport-building at the top of a cold call is why
you're getting hung up on."
**Beats:** why "how's your day going" signals salesperson instantly · rapport is
*earned* after relevance, not before · what to lead with instead · the reframe.
**Payoff:** order matters more than content — relevance, then rapport.
**Final frame:** the two orders, side by side, one crossed out.

## 9. SCRIPT — "The pattern interrupt that still works"
**Hook** (forbidden): "This one's overused and it still works, which tells you
something."
**Beats:** the line · why it works mechanically (breaks the script-detection
reflex) · why overuse hasn't killed it · the version that has died.
**Payoff:** honest nuance — most content won't admit when a cliché works.
**Final frame:** live/dead versions split.

## 10. PSYCH — "Why you sound nervous even when you're not"
**Hook** (identity callout): "If people ask if you're okay on calls, it's not
nerves. It's one habit."
**Beats:** upward inflection at the end of statements · demo the same sentence
both ways · why it reads as asking permission · the 10-second drill.
**Payoff:** the audio demo. Hearing it beats reading it.
**Final frame:** the drill instruction.

## 11. TEARDOWN — "The gatekeeper call, annotated"
**Hook** (receipt-first): Play 6 seconds of the gatekeeper interaction cold. Then:
"Three mistakes. Watch again."
**Beats:** replay with annotations popping in · each mistake named · the rebuild ·
final clean version.
**Payoff:** annotated replay format — highly rewatchable.
**Final frame:** clean version playing, silent captions.

## 12. OBJECTION — "We already have a vendor"
**Hook** (specificity): "This objection is true 90% of the time. That's exactly
why it's beatable."
**Beats:** don't attack the incumbent · the question that finds the gap · why
"what would have to change" outperforms "what don't you like" · script it.
**Payoff:** the exact question.
**Final frame:** the question, on screen.

## 13. SERIES — "Day 7. The one that went badly."
**Hook** (open loop): "Day 7. This is the call I didn't want to post."
**Beats:** unedited failure · the moment it went wrong, timestamped · what should
have happened · scoreboard.
**Payoff:** genuine vulnerability with a lesson attached — high-arousal, brand-safe.
**Final frame:** scoreboard `7/100`.

## 14. CONTRARIAN — "Your CRM is making you worse"
**Hook** (loss framing): "Logging every call is quietly destroying your call quality."
**Beats:** attention split between talking and typing · the listening cost · what
to log and when · the 30-second-after rule.
**Payoff:** a workflow change, not a platitude.
**Final frame:** the rule.

## 15. SCRIPT — "How to leave a voicemail that gets returned"
**Hook** (specificity): "Voicemail return rate: about 4%. Mine's higher and it's
because of one structural thing."
**Beats:** most voicemails pitch · the open-loop voicemail · 14 seconds max ·
number twice, slow, front and back.
**Payoff:** full script, timed on screen.
**Final frame:** the script, 14-second timer running.

## 16. PSYCH — "The silence that closes"
**Hook** (contradiction): "The best thing you can say after your pitch is nothing.
For four full seconds."
**Beats:** demo four seconds of dead air on screen (uncomfortable to watch = good
retention) · why filling it undoes the ask · who speaks first loses · practice drill.
**Payoff:** the four-second silence *shown* — the discomfort is the lesson.
**Final frame:** timer counting 4 seconds.

## 17. TEARDOWN — "The best cold call I've ever heard"
**Hook** (superlative + receipt): "This is the best cold open I've heard. It's 9
seconds long."
**Beats:** play it · break down each phrase · what's absent (no permission ask, no
rapport, no name-drop) · why absence is the skill.
**Payoff:** subtraction as technique.
**Final frame:** the 9 seconds replaying.

## 18. OBJECTION — "How did you get my number?"
**Hook** (identity callout): "This one makes everyone panic. It's actually the
easiest one on the list."
**Beats:** panic response vs. flat honest response · demo both · why honesty
disarms · one-line answer + immediate pivot.
**Payoff:** the exact answer.
**Final frame:** the line.

## 19. CONTRARIAN — "Cold calling isn't dead, your list is"
**Hook** (contradiction): "Cold calling isn't dead. You're just calling people who
were never going to buy."
**Beats:** connect rate vs. conversion rate · the list-quality math · 50 good
numbers beat 500 scraped ones · how to tell in 20 seconds.
**Payoff:** the math, on screen.
**Final frame:** the two columns.

## 20. SCRIPT — "The callback line"
**Hook** (open loop): "They said call back in three months. Here's the line that
makes that actually happen."
**Beats:** why "I'll follow up" evaporates · the specific-commitment technique ·
scripting the calendar ask · the exact words.
**Payoff:** the line + the calendar mechanic.
**Final frame:** the line.

## 21. PSYCH — "Rejection isn't personal, but your body doesn't know that"
**Hook** (identity callout): "Twenty no's in a row does something physical to you.
Here's the reset."
**Beats:** the physiological reality · why call 21 is worse than call 1 · the
90-second reset protocol · the streak rule.
**Payoff:** an actual protocol, not "stay positive."
**Final frame:** the protocol, 3 steps.

## 22. TEARDOWN — "Reading a real rejection, word for word"
**Hook** (receipt-first): Screen-record of the actual message. Read it verbatim,
no framing, for the first 8 seconds.
**Beats:** read it cold · then: "here's what he's actually saying" · translate
each line · the response that would have worked.
**Payoff:** the translation. Verbatim-then-decode is a strong, honest format.
**Final frame:** the response draft.

## 23. SERIES — "Day 15. Halfway. Numbers so far."
**Hook** (specificity): "Halfway. 51 calls. 4 meetings. Here's what actually moved
the number."
**Beats:** real data on screen · what changed at call 30 · what I stopped doing ·
scoreboard.
**Payoff:** real numbers including the bad ones.
**Final frame:** scoreboard `51/100`.

## 24. OBJECTION — "It's too expensive"
**Hook** (contradiction): "'Too expensive' almost never means too expensive."
**Beats:** it means unclear value or wrong buyer · the diagnostic · never discount
first · the question that separates the two.
**Payoff:** the diagnostic question.
**Final frame:** the question.

## 25. SCRIPT — "Opening for a referral call"
**Hook** (specificity): "Referral calls convert about 5x cold ones and most people
open them exactly wrong."
**Beats:** lead with the name, not yourself · the first 6 words · why "X suggested
I call" beats "I'm calling from" · full script.
**Payoff:** the script.
**Final frame:** the 6 words.

## 26. CONTRARIAN — "Talk less. Way less."
**Hook** (specificity): "Best-performing calls: rep talks about 43% of the time.
Most reps talk 70%+."
**Beats:** the ratio · why more talking feels productive and isn't · how to shut
up mechanically (count to 2 before responding) · the drill.
**Payoff:** a countable behavior.
**Final frame:** 43% vs 70%, big.

## 27. PSYCH — "Why your tone matters more than your script"
**Hook** (visual cold open): Same sentence, three tones, back to back, no
explanation for the first 6 seconds.
**Beats:** let the audio do it · then name what changed · downward inflection =
certainty · the one-line rewrite that fixes most calls.
**Payoff:** the audio demo.
**Final frame:** the three waveforms.

## 28. TEARDOWN — "30 seconds of a call that should have been 8"
**Hook** (loss framing): "Everything before the 22-second mark here is wasted.
Watch."
**Beats:** play the full 30 · timestamp the moment it should have started · cut to
the 8-second version · compare.
**Payoff:** the cut version.
**Final frame:** 8-second version looping.

## 29. OBJECTION — "Call me back next quarter"
**Hook** (contradiction): "Next quarter is a no with a calendar invite attached."
**Beats:** the real meaning · the one question that tests it · what to do if real ·
what to do if not.
**Payoff:** the test question.
**Final frame:** the question.

## 30. SERIES — "Day 30. Final numbers. Including what failed."
**Hook** (specificity): "100 calls. 30 days. 9 meetings. 2 closed. And 4 things
that completely didn't work."
**Beats:** full data on screen · the 4 failures named specifically · the one thing
that mattered most · what's next.
**Payoff:** the failures. Everyone posts wins; posting the failures is the
differentiator and it costs nothing but ego.
**Final frame:** final scoreboard `100/100`. Next series teased.

## Production notes

- **Scripts 5, 13, 23, 30** are the SERIES spine — they run across the 30 days and
  each one is an open loop that renews for free. Start this on day one; the
  numbered scoreboard graphic becomes the visual brand.
- **Scripts 1, 11, 17, 22, 28** are TEARDOWNS — the highest-value, most defensible
  format. Needs real call audio. Get consent or use your own outbound calls.
- **Script 3** is the thesis post. Pin it. It explains why this page exists and
  why the person running it understands attention.
- Every OBJECTION script is a 30-second answer to a question people literally
  search. These are the YouTube Shorts plays — keyword-loaded titles, long tail.
- Rotate pillars daily. Never two of the same pillar back to back.

## Legal/consent note on call audio

Recording consent law varies — some US states require all-party consent, and the
strictest applicable jurisdiction governs. Use your own outbound calls with
disclosure, get written permission before publishing anyone's voice, or
reconstruct calls with a stated "recreated" label. A reconstruction labeled as
one is honest; an unlabeled one is a fabricated receipt and is on the red list.
