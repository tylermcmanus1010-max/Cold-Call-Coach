# Account Setup — Human Checklist

Every step here requires a human. All five platforms prohibit automated account
creation and enforce it with SMS/CAPTCHA verification. Attempting to automate it
gets accounts banned and can poison the phone number and IP for future signups.
Budget ~40 minutes total.

## Before you touch a signup page

- [ ] **Lock the niche.** Everything below depends on it.
- [ ] **Pick the handle.** Check availability on all five *simultaneously* at
      namecheckr.com or similar. Take the one that's free everywhere, even if it's
      your third choice. Cross-platform handle consistency is worth more than the
      perfect name.
- [ ] Handle rules: under 15 characters (X's limit is the binding constraint), no
      numbers/underscores if avoidable, sayable out loud, no year in it.
- [ ] **Dedicated email.** One address for all five. Not your personal.
- [ ] **Phone number** for verification. A real one — most VOIP numbers are
      rejected now.
- [ ] Decide the brand kit: 1 avatar, 1 banner, 2 fonts, 3 colors. Consistent
      across all five so a viewer who sees you twice recognizes you.

## Order of creation

Do it in this order — it lets you reuse assets and catch handle conflicts early:

1. **YouTube** (Google account → channel). Slowest, most setup. Do it first.
2. **TikTok** — highest discovery value, get it aging immediately.
3. **Instagram** — creator account, not personal.
4. **Facebook** — Page linked to Instagram via Meta Business Suite. Do this
      together with Instagram; it enables cross-posting Reels.
5. **X** — fastest, least setup.

## Per-account settings that matter

**All:**
- [ ] Switch to Creator/Business/Professional account type where offered
- [ ] Bio formula: `what you do for whom` + `proof or credential` + `one CTA`
- [ ] Same avatar and handle everywhere

**YouTube:** enable Shorts, set channel keywords, create a Community tab post,
set up a channel trailer slot (leave empty until you have a real one)

**TikTok:** turn off "allow downloads" only if you don't want clippers — for a
growing page, leave downloads **on**, it's free distribution (this is the Speed
lesson). Enable comment filters for slurs.

**Instagram:** connect to the Facebook Page, enable Reels cross-posting, turn on
manual comment filtering

**Facebook:** Page (not profile), category set, Meta Business Suite linked

**X:** enable the alt-text prompt, set the pinned post slot

## Warm-up (first 7 days — do not skip)

New accounts posting at full volume on day one look like bots and get
reach-limited before they publish anything good.

- Days 1–2: no posting. Browse the feed, follow 20–30 accounts in the niche,
  like and comment genuinely. Let the algorithm build a profile of you.
- Days 3–4: 1 post/day.
- Days 5–7: 2 posts/day.
- Day 8+: full cadence per `03-PLATFORM-PLAYBOOKS.md`.

## Monetization prerequisites (set up now, unlock later)

- [ ] Tax info on file (YouTube AdSense, TikTok, Meta) — do this early, it takes
      days to verify and blocks payouts
- [ ] Bank/payout method on each platform
- [ ] A landing page or link-in-bio destination so traffic has somewhere to go
      from day one. Traffic you can't capture is traffic you lose.
