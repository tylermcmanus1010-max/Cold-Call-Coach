## What to collect from him

Four things, in order of how much they matter:

1. **His CSLB licence number.** California requires a licensed contractor to
   put their licence number on all advertising, and a website counts. Right now
   the page says "Licensed, bonded and insured" with no number behind it —
   which is both a legal problem and a wasted trust signal. Ask first, it is the
   single most credible thing a roofer can put on a page.

2. **Photos. More than anything else on this list.** Roofing is the most visual
   trade there is and nobody can see their own roof. Ask for:
   - Before and after on the same job, three or four jobs
   - A tile re-roof and a shingle re-roof if he has both
   - His truck, and him or his crew on a roof
   
   Send them to me and I will size and embed them. `./cc photo` handles it.

3. **Hours and service area.** I put 7–5 weekdays, Saturday by appointment,
   Sunday emergencies, and ten North County towns. All of it is my guess.

4. **Three reviews with real names.** The only genuine gap left on the page. I
   will not write them.
