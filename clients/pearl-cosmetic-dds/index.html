About

Dr. Nasrin Mojaver

We perform all aspects of dentistry, with a focus on healthy, well-designed smiles. We also practise holistic dentistry — non-toxic materials chosen so that what goes in your mouth does not work against the rest of you.

Dr. Nasrin Mojaver has over 18 years of experience, and has invested in the equipment that actually changes how a visit feels: a Solea laser that does most fillings with no needle and no drill, CEREC 3D so a crown is finished the same day, and an iTero scanner so nobody has to bite into a tray of paste.

If you have been avoiding the dentist, this is the practice built for you.

  • Most fillings done with a laser — no needle, no drill, no noise
  • Crowns designed and fitted in one appointment
  • Digital scanning instead of impression moulds
  • Non-toxic, biocompatible materials
  • Sedation and relaxation options for anxious patients
  • Membership from $399 a year if you have no insurance
