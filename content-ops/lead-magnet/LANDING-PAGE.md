# Landing Page + Email Capture — Build Sheet

**Stack:** Kit (kit.com, formerly ConvertKit) free tier. Everything below is
copy-paste ready.

## Why Kit and not Carrd

Carrd's free and Pro Lite tiers exclude forms *and* embeds — a free Carrd page
physically cannot capture an email. Forms require Pro Standard at $19/yr.

Kit's free tier covers 10,000 subscribers with one landing page, one form, and
one automation — exactly the shape of this job — and it hosts and delivers the
PDF itself. One system, $0.

Revisit Carrd when you want a custom domain and a designed page. Then it's
$19/yr and you embed the Kit form into it. The copy below ports unchanged.

---

## Setup, in order

1. **kit.com → sign up free.** Use the Cold Open Gmail.
2. **Grow → Landing Pages & Forms → Create → Landing Page.** Pick any minimal
   template — you're replacing all the copy anyway. Recommended: a plain
   centered one, no stock photography.
3. **Style it to brand:** background `#0B0B0C`, text `#FAFAFA`, button
   `#FF3B30` with `#FAFAFA` text. Headline font: pick the heaviest sans in the
   list. Upload `assets/brand/avatar-400.png` as the logo.
4. **Paste the copy below.**
5. **Grow → Incentive email:** turn ON "Send incentive email", upload
   `The-Objection-Pack.pdf` as the download, paste the email copy below.
6. **Settings → the page URL.** Set the slug to `objection-pack`. You'll get
   something like `coldopen.kit.com/objection-pack`.
7. **Put that URL in all five bios.**

## Landing page copy

**Headline**
```
"Not interested" isn't an objection. It's a reflex.
```

**Subhead**
```
12 cold call objections, what they actually mean, and the exact words
that beat them. 15 pages. Free.
```

**Body — what's inside**
```
What each objection actually means. It's almost never what they said.

The exact script for all twelve. Word for word, not principles.

What never to say — and why those lines end the call on the spot.

The three-step pattern underneath all of it, so you can build a response
```

## Why the copy is shaped this way

- **The headline is a hook from the pack itself**, not a description of the
  pack. Same principle as the video hooks: state the contradiction, don't
  announce the topic. "Download our free guide" is a topic announcement.
- **"15 pages. Free."** — the number is the credibility and the word "free"
  removes the price objection before it forms. Both are load-bearing.
- **The bullets say what's inside, not what you'll achieve.** "Close more
  deals" is what every sales grifter promises. "Word for word, not principles"
  is a claim only someone with actual content can make.
- **"Start with #12"** in the delivery email drives the first open into a
  specific page. People who read one page read more. People who file the PDF
  never open it.

## When traffic starts arriving

Track conversion rate on the page — Kit reports it. Benchmarks for a cold
short-form audience:

| Rate | Read |
|---|---|
| Under 15% | Headline or offer mismatch. The traffic wanted something else. |
| 20–35% | Normal and healthy. |
| Over 40% | The offer is strong — make more content pointing here. |

If the rate is fine but signups are low, the problem is upstream: the videos
aren't sending people. If the rate is low and traffic is high, the problem is
this page.
