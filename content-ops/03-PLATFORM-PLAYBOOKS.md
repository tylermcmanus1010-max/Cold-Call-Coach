# Platform Playbooks (as of August 2026)

Same idea, five different jobs. Cross-posting identical files to all five is the
most common beginner mistake — each platform's algorithm reads native behavior
and penalizes obvious re-uploads (visible watermarks especially).

## The role of each platform

| Platform | Job in the funnel | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Discovery + audience research | Highest cold-reach for unknown accounts. Comments are free market research. |
| **Instagram Reels** | Conversion + community | Best follow-through and DM conversation. Where a follower becomes a relationship. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Search + longevity + revenue | Only platform where content compounds. Shorts feed a long-form channel. Highest RPM path. |
| **X** | Text-native leverage + credibility | Repurpose scripts as threads. Cheapest place to test *ideas* before spending production on them. |
| **Facebook** | Volume + underrated payout | Least competition per view. Older demographic, high reach on Reels, genuinely worth the 5 minutes of cross-posting. |

## Specs

| | TikTok | Reels | Shorts | X | FB Reels |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aspect | 9:16 | 9:16 | 9:16 | 9:16 / 1:1 | 9:16 |
| Resolution | 1080×1920 | 1080×1920 | 1080×1920 | 1080×1920 | 1080×1920 |
| Sweet spot | 15–34s | 30–60s | 60–90s | 20–45s | 30–60s |
| Monetizable length | >60s | n/a (bonus programs) | any (Shorts feed) | any | >60s |
| Caption safe zone | avoid bottom 20%, right 15% | avoid bottom 25% | avoid bottom 15% | full | avoid bottom 25% |
| Hashtags | 3–5 | 3–5 | 2–3 | 1–2 | 2–3 |

**Universal:** burn captions into the video. Most viewing is sound-off on first
watch. Never rely on platform auto-captions for the hook line.

## Monetization thresholds (verify before relying on these — they move)

**YouTube** — two paths as of 2026:
- 1,000 subscribers **+ 10M Shorts views in 90 days**, OR
- 1,000 subscribers **+ 4,000 watch hours** (long-form)

The dual-tier structure makes a Shorts-first strategy genuinely viable for a
faceless channel now, which was not true a couple of years ago. Note the Shorts
bar is steep — 10M/90 days is ~111k views/day. The long-form path is usually the
faster door, which argues for pairing Shorts with a weekly long-form upload.

**TikTok Creator Rewards:**
- 10,000 followers, 100,000 valid views in 30 days, 18+, eligible country
- **Pays only on original content over 1 minute** — this single rule should shape
  your format choices
- "Qualified views" = For You feed, 5+ seconds, unique accounts
- Realistic rate: roughly $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views. A 2026 sample of
  disclosed creator figures ran ~$362–$1,035 per million qualified views,
  averaging near $800.
- LIVE Gifts unlock much earlier at 1,000 followers

**Reality check on ad revenue:** at those rates, a million views a month is a few
hundred dollars. Platform payouts are not the business. They're a scoreboard.

## Where the money actually is

Ranked by realistic yield for a faceless page:

1. **Your own product/service** — highest margin by an order of magnitude, and
   the audience is qualified by the content itself
2. **Affiliate** — works well in high-intent niches (software, tools, gear)
3. **Sponsorships** — needs ~10k+ engaged followers; brand-safety matters here,
   which is the concrete cost of a shock-heavy strategy
4. **Platform ad revenue** — last, and CPM varies ~50x by niche. Finance/B2B
   ($25–50 CPM) vs. gaming/entertainment ($1–4). **Niche choice determines
   earnings more than any other single decision** — more than volume, more than
   quality.

## Posting cadence

| Platform | Daily target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 2–3 | Highest volume tolerance; test hooks here |
| Reels | 1–2 | Over-posting suppresses; quality-weighted |
| Shorts | 1–2 | Plus 1 long-form/week |
| X | 3–5 posts + 1 thread | Text is cheap; ideas tested here first |
| FB Reels | 1–2 | Straight cross-post from Reels is acceptable here |

**Total: 8–14 pieces/day from roughly 3–4 original ideas.** The multiplier comes
from re-cutting, not from writing more.

## Cross-posting rules

1. **Never post a watermarked file.** Export clean from the editor; download from
   TikTok only as a last resort and never with the watermark.
2. **Re-upload natively.** No auto-share links between platforms — reach is
   throttled.
3. **Rewrite the caption per platform.** TikTok = conversational bait. Reels =
   value + CTA. Shorts = keyword-loaded for search. X = the hook as standalone
   text. FB = plain and direct.
4. **Stagger 2–4 hours.** Simultaneous identical uploads look automated.
5. **Re-cut, don't re-post.** Different hook, different length, different opening
   frame for the highest-value ideas.

## The repurposing pipeline

```
1 core idea
  └── 1 long-form (YouTube, 6–10 min)
        ├── 5–7 vertical clips  → TikTok / Reels / Shorts / FB
        ├── 1 thread            → X
        ├── 3 standalone posts  → X
        └── 1 carousel          → Instagram
```

One genuinely good idea, developed properly, is 10–14 pieces of content. This is
the whole efficiency argument: don't produce more ideas, extract more from each.
