## Division of labor

**Requires you (human), cannot be delegated:**
- Creating the five accounts (SMS verification, CAPTCHA, ToS acceptance — all
  five platforms prohibit automated signup; attempting it gets accounts banned
  at scale and can burn the phone number/IP for future signups)
- Linking bank/tax info for monetization
- Recording any original audio/footage
- Final publish button (or authorizing a scheduler that holds the tokens)

**I can do, every day:**
- Trend reconnaissance and the daily brief
- Scripting: hooks, beats, VO copy, on-screen text, captions
- Title/description/hashtag sets per platform
- Thumbnail concepts and text
- Performance analysis from exported CSVs
- Iterating the hook library against what actually lands

**Possible with setup:** a scheduler (Buffer/Metricool/Publer, or the official
APIs) can automate publishing once *you* have authorized it with your own
credentials. That is the legitimate automation path. Bot-created accounts are not.
