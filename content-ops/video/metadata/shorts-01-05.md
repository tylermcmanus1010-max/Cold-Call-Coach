## Hashtag notes

Three to five per platform, never more. The set rotates deliberately —
identical tags on every post is a spam signal.

**Core (always):** `#coldcalling` `#sdr`
**Rotate in:** `#salestips` `#b2bsales` `#prospecting` `#sdrlife`
`#salestraining` `#salesdevelopment` `#objectionhandling` `#closing`

X takes 1–2 max, and often reads better with none.

Comment velocity in the first hour is a ranking input on every platform here.
Reply to every comment on a new post. Pin the reply that best restates the hook
or opens a debate — a pinned argument keeps the thread alive.
