How we decide

We say no to applications, and we'd rather be upfront about that than waste your time. Our capacity is finite, and everything that leaves the floor carries our name.

    Three minutes. Tell us what you sell, what you'd want made, and why you want to work with us.

  2. We read it

    A person on our team reads every application, usually within two business days.

  3. A video call

    Every serious application includes one. We want to understand what you're building and whether we're the right manufacturer for it. It goes both ways — bring your questions.

    Accepted, waitlisted, or declined, with a reason. Accepted members get portal credentials the same day and can order against any quote they've uploaded.
