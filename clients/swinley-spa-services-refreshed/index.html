About

What a service visit actually includes

Most people meet their spa company once and never see the same face again. That is how a tub ends up with cloudy water, a waterlogged cover and a heater nobody looked at until January.

Every visit is the same list, every time, and it takes about half an hour. You get a photo of the water and the chemistry readings on your phone before we drive away — so you know we came, and you know exactly where your water stands.

The tub is the thing you actually use in a Nebraska winter, so spa service runs all year. Pools run on the season, and the opening and closing visits either side of it are the two that matter most — a pool closed properly in October is a pool that costs nothing in April.

  • Test and balance bromine or chlorine, pH and alkalinity
  • Rinse the filter and check it for tears
  • Wipe the shell, waterline and jets
  • Clean and condition the cover
  • Check the heater, pump, blower and control panel
  • Through winter, check the cover seal and freeze protection
  • Top the water back up to level
  • Photo and readings sent to you before we leave
