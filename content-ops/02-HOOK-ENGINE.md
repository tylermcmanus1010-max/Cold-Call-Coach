# The Hook Engine

The scroll is the enemy. Everything here exists to beat the thumb.

## The retention architecture

Retention is not one hook. It is a hook plus a re-hook every few seconds. The
leaked 36-page MrBeast production memo is explicit about this: retention "drops
off most sharply" in the opening stretch, so the opening is engineered to
front-load the most compelling material, and a deliberate **re-engagement** beat
is scheduled before attention can sag. Scaled down from a 20-minute video to a
45-second vertical, the same skeleton holds:

| Time | Job | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.5s | Motion before meaning. Something must already be moving or changing on frame 1. | Static talking-head cold open. Dead. |
| 0.5–3s | The promise or the open loop. State the payoff or open a question the brain must close. | Preamble. "Hey guys, so today..." |
| 3–8s | **First payoff.** Deliver something real. This is the trust tax — pay it early. | Stalling until the "real" content. |
| 8–20s | Escalation. Each beat must be larger, stranger, or more specific than the last. | Flat middle. The most common death. |
| every 5–7s | Re-hook: cut, angle change, text pop, sound shift, or new sub-loop. | Same shot for 15 seconds. |
| final 3s | Loop-back or hard stop. Last frame should invite a replay or a comment. | Outro. "Like and subscribe." Never. |

**Zero dull moments** is the operating rule. If a beat does not advance the loop,
raise the stakes, or land a joke, it gets cut. When in doubt, cut it.

## Rule of thumb: the 3-second contract

Whatever you promise in the first 3 seconds, you are contractually obligated to
deliver by the end. Breaking that contract is how you get watch-time without
followers — people finish the video, feel cheated, and never follow. Views
without follows means the hook is writing checks the body can't cash.

## Hook taxonomy — 10 families

Every hook worth using is a variant of one of these. Rotate families
deliberately; never run the same family twice in a row on the same account.

### 1. Contradiction
Attack a belief the viewer holds.
- "Everything you've been told about ___ is backwards."
- "___ is not hard. You're just doing it in the wrong order."
- "The advice that ruined ___ for an entire generation."

### 2. Open loop
Start a story mid-collapse and refuse to resolve it yet.
- "...and that's when he hung up on me."
- "I lost $___ in 11 minutes. Here's the exact moment it happened."
- "Watch what happens at 14 seconds."

### 3. Loss / negative framing
Loss aversion outperforms gain framing. People will watch to avoid a mistake
they won't watch to get a win.
- "Stop doing ___ right now."
- "This one habit is costing you ___."
- "You're about to make the same mistake I did."

### 4. Extreme specificity
Numbers are credibility. Round numbers read as invented; odd ones read as real.
- "$4,712 in 9 days."
- "I sent 1,847 cold emails. 3 worked. Here's what those 3 had."
- "It takes exactly 41 seconds."

### 5. Identity callout
Name the viewer. The brain flags self-relevant information involuntarily.
- "If you're ___, this is going to sting."
- "This is for the people who ___."
- "You already know if this is about you."

### 6. Forbidden knowledge
Frame ordinary information as suppressed. Use sparingly — it's the fastest hook
family to burn your credibility.
- "Nobody in ___ will say this out loud."
- "They train us not to tell you this."
- "I'm going to get in trouble for this one."

### 7. Visual cold open
No words at all. The image does the hooking. Strongest family on TikTok and
Reels, and the most under-used by beginners.
- Something breaking, pouring, collapsing, transforming.
- A screen with an absurd number on it.
- A hand doing something the viewer can't immediately parse.

### 8. Stakes declaration
Put a clock or a consequence on it.
- "I have 30 seconds to change your mind."
- "If this doesn't work, I'm deleting the account."
- "One take. No edits."

### 9. Receipt-first
Show the proof, then explain it. Inverts the normal claim→evidence order and
buys instant credibility.
- Open on the dashboard, the invoice, the message, the result. Then talk.

### 10. Ranked opinion
Lists trigger completion instinct and comment disagreement simultaneously.
- "Ranking ___ from worst to best. Number 1 will annoy people."
- "The 3 ___ that actually matter. The rest is noise."

## 50 fill-in templates

Swap `[X]` for your niche noun. These are scaffolding, not scripture — the
specificity you add is what makes them work.

**Contradiction**

3. "The reason [X] failed for you has nothing to do with [obvious cause]."
4. "Everyone gets [X] wrong in the exact same way."
5. "[Authority figure] is wrong about [X] and I can prove it in 40 seconds."

**Open loop**
6. "He said seven words and the whole thing fell apart."
7. "This is the part nobody films."
8. "I almost didn't post this."
9. "Keep watching. It gets worse."
10. "The second one is the one that got me."

**Loss framing**
11. "Delete this from your [X] immediately."
12. "This is costing you [specific number] a month and you can't see it."
13. "If you do [X] before [Y], you've already lost."
14. "Three words that kill [X] instantly."
15. "You have about [N] days before this stops working."

**Specificity**
16. "[Odd number] attempts. [Small number] worked."
17. "It cost me $[odd amount] to learn this."
18. "[N] seconds is the entire difference."
19. "I tracked [X] for [N] days. The data is not what I expected."
20. "97% of [group] do this. It's the 3% that [outcome]."

**Identity callout**
21. "If you've ever [relatable failure], this one's for you."
22. "[Group], I need 20 seconds."
23. "You're not lazy. You're [reframe]."
24. "This will only make sense if you've [experience]."
25. "Tag someone who does this."

**Forbidden knowledge**
26. "Nobody who's actually good at [X] talks about this."
27. "This is the part they leave out of the course."
28. "I worked in [industry]. Here's what actually happens."
29. "You were never supposed to see this."
30. "Screenshot this before it gets taken down." *(only if literally true)*

**Visual cold open**
31. Open on the result. Silent. Text appears at 1.5s.
32. Open mid-action — already pouring, already typing, already falling.
33. Open on a number so large or small it demands explanation.
34. Open on a face reacting to something offscreen.
35. Open on a mess, then rewind to how it happened.

**Stakes**
36. "One take. If I mess up, it stays in."
37. "I have [N] seconds. Timer starts now."
38. "If this flops I'm never doing [X] again."
39. "Doing this until it works. Day [N]."
40. "$[amount] on the line."

**Receipt-first**
41. Screen recording first, commentary second.
42. "This is real. Here's the proof." → cut to proof within 2s.
43. Show the before/after split before saying a single word.
44. "That's the actual [document/dashboard/message]. Now let me explain."
45. Read the message out loud verbatim before framing it.

**Ranked**
46. "Worst to best. Fight me in the comments."
47. "Tier list. Number [N] is going to upset people."
48. "Three that work. Two that don't. One that's a scam."
49. "I ranked [N] [X]. The winner surprised me."
50. "Every [X], ranked by how much they cost you."

## Hook testing protocol

Hooks are the highest-leverage variable and the cheapest to test.

1. Write **3 hooks for every script.** Never one.
2. Post the same body with different hooks to different platforms same day.
3. The metric is **3-second retention** (TikTok/Reels) or **viewed vs. swiped
   away** (Shorts). Not likes. Not comments. Retention at 3 seconds.
4. Anything under ~50% at 3s: the hook failed, the video is not the problem.
5. Log every hook and its 3s number in `tools/tracker.csv`. After 30 posts you
   will have a personal hook ranking that beats any generic list — including
   this one. That is the point.

## What kills hooks

- Any word before the hook. No "hey guys", no logo sting, no intro animation.
- Explaining what the video will be about instead of just starting it.
- Audio that starts quiet and ramps. Start at full level.
- Text that requires more than ~1 second to read on frame 1.
- Burying the most interesting frame at 0:12. Move it to 0:00 and rebuild around it.
