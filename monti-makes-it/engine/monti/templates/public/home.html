The usual chain

Buying from us

You and the line that makes your product. One margin, and you can see what it is applied to.

We do not publish a savings percentage, because a percentage without a baseline is not a number — it is a claim. What we do instead: once you are a member, every item you order carries its own savings ledger, measured against a baseline you supply and we record — your previous supplier's quote, or your documented landed cost. Where there is no baseline we can source, that item shows no savings figure at all.
