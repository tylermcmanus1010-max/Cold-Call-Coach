About

Two trucks, one family, twenty-two years

Bayview Plumbing has been fixing San Diego homes since 2003. Ray started with one truck and a phone number written on the door; today it's still small enough that the person who answers is the person who shows up.

We don't sell you a repipe when you need a washer. You get a flat price before any work starts, and if we can't fix it, you don't pay for the visit.
